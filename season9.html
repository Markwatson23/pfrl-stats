What's verified — and what's not

Verified: Texas feature race and sprint classifications, and Monza feature race classification, including drivers, teams, grid positions and race statuses shown in the original graphics. Texas qualifying also exists in the source archive, but is not yet transcribed here.

Not verified: Complete classifications for the other championship rounds, complete Season 9 race-by-race points, and final championship totals. The standings graphics in the archive are snapshots headed Japan (drivers) and Italy (constructors), not proven final standings.

Pre-season Spa and Abu Dhabi events are excluded from championship records. Season 9 results are not yet included in career totals, achievement awards, championship rankings or teammate comparisons.
